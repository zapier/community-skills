# Review memo

The orchestrator owns the synthesis. Scale the memo to the piece and stage: for a short stub, combine brief sections and report only material findings. Preserve the assessment, assumptions, execution limitations, applicable evidence, and decisions. Never hide a failed or incomplete review lens.

## Full mode

```markdown
# Content Council review: [title]

**Assessment:** [Ready for the next review / Revise / Do not proceed / Conditional / Incomplete]. [One reason and any limit on the assessment.]
**Execution:** [Independent parallel lenses / sequential simulated lenses / mixed, with any missing lens].
**Piece:** [format, surface, stage] | **Desired outcome:** [value or unknown]

## Decisions needed
- [Question, proposed decision owner or unassigned, and whether it blocks readiness.] 
- [None, if none.]

## Audience and review bar
[Seven profile fields with Confirmed / Inferred / Unknown / Not applicable and evidence.]
**Register:** [x] | **Proof bar:** [x] | **Travel test:** [x or not applicable] | **Watch for:** [x]
[State applicable user-provided standards and any unresolved conflicts. Mark load-bearing assumptions and all audience-dependent findings PROVISIONAL if conditional.]

## Priority fixes
### Must-fix
- [Location and exact quote, or specific missing element] | [problem and why it blocks] | [correction direction] | [standard number] | [owner or decision needed if applicable].
### Should-fix
- [Same evidence and standard, scaled to importance.]
### Optional
- [Same evidence and standard, scaled to importance.]

## Travel test
**Surface:** [verbatim title, headings, emphasis, lists, captions, CTA where visible, or note that formatting was unavailable.]
**Test:** [named test]. **Result:** [pass / fail / not assessed / not applicable].
[What a skimmer gets and where meaning breaks.]

## Evidence and attribution
- [Claim and location] | [verified against source / cited but unchecked / unsupported / not verifiable] | [source and what was checked, or gap tag].

## Lens outcomes and trade-offs
[Brief outcome for each lens, including no material finding where applicable. Name actual disagreements, the decision taken, and why. State missing lenses or fallbacks.]

## Strengths to preserve
- [Specific strength with an exact quote or location.]

## Next steps and readiness
[Named remaining blockers, unresolved decisions, and separate fact-check, specialist review, or approval if needed. No final readiness verdict for conditional or incomplete reviews.]
```

## Quick mode

```markdown
# Quick Content Council review: [title]
**Status:** [conditional / ready for next review / revise / incomplete] and one reason.
**Audience and assumptions:** [primary reader, authority, surface, what is confirmed or provisional].
**Travel test:** [test, verbatim visible surface, result and limitation].
**Top fixes:** [up to three findings with location, evidence, severity and standard].
**Proof risk:** [high-impact claim needing verification, if any; say this was not a full proof audit].
**Decision or next step:** [question, owner if known, or next action].
```

A negative assessment such as Revise or Do not proceed may be issued when must-fixes remain. Do not declare a piece ready for publication while audience assumptions remain unconfirmed, a required lens is missing, or a must-fix remains. Ready for the next review is stage-specific and is not publication clearance. A readiness assessment is not publication approval.
